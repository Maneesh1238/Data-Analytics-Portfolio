## Retail Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview

The "Retail Analysis Dashboard" provides key insights into customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and subscription patterns.
It combines data from a **MySQL database** and presents it in an interactive **Power BI** dashboard, helping retail stakeholders identify trends and make data-driven decisions.

## Objectives

* Understand customer behavior and purchasing patterns.
* Analyze sales performance by **category**, **age group**, and **gender**.
* Compare revenue contribution from **different customer segments**.
* Measure subscription rates and customer engagement metrics.

## 🗂️ Dataset Details

  Source:  MySQL Database (`customer_behaviour`)
  Table Used: `retail_analysis`

## 💡 Key Insights

* 4K total customers analyzed.
* Average rating: 3.75 stars.
* Average purchase amount:** ~$59.76K.
* 27% of customers have active subscriptions.
* Clothing category leads both in sales count and total revenue.
* Young adults and **middle-aged customers** contribute the highest purchase value.
* Female customers have a higher subscription rate (68%) compared to males.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* **Filters (Slicers):** Gender, Category, Shipping Type, Subscription Status.
* **KPIs:** Total Customers, Average Review Rating, Average Purchase Amount.
* **Visuals:**

  * Bar charts for Sales and Revenue by Category.
  * Donut charts for Subscription Distribution and Gender-based Subscriptions.
  * Bar chart for Revenue by Age Group.
